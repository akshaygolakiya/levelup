## Bugs found and fixed

1. Saved screen printed "[object HTMLElement]": native `Node.append` does not flatten arrays (and printed "null" for null children, e.g. the Settings storage notice). Added `put()` in util.js and used it everywhere.
2. Word session crashed on completion (words.js): `back` const shadowed the deck option (TDZ), leaving a blank page after the last card. Renamed.
3. Non-ASCII words saved wrongly: selecting "cafe" with an accent saved "caf" (ASCII-only trim). Now Unicode-aware.
4. Grammar check was strict about a trailing space after punctuation ("  Merged. " marked wrong). Normalisation fixed.
5. Weekly renderer crashed on non-array sections; now tolerant.
6. Streak rule implemented: kept/done >= ceil(7n/10) of core (10 of 13 for day 1; 7 of 10 passes, 6 of 10 fails); skipped no longer blocks. Only days finished on their own calendar day extend the streak; catch-up completions never extend or break it (E8).
7. Two tabs overwrote each other: added a `storage` listener to adopt the other tab state; state is also flushed at once when the tab is hidden (background timers were delayed by seconds).
8. Streak record is recomputed on Today load (after imports/edits).
9. Empty day said "Everything finished"; now "This day has no items".
10. Layout: archive calendar overflowed at 360px; touch targets under 44px (segmented controls, small icon buttons, grammar blanks, calendar cells); long unbroken words/RTL now wrap; card stack back cards showed through the top card; the sticky action bar in the expanded card was translucent over text.
11. Added a catch-up "Off" option and an "original link not available" note for items without url.

## Not fixed / limits

- A day counts for the streak only if its 70% threshold is reached before midnight of that day (finishing after midnight is catch-up).
- Two tabs: state is adopted from storage events but the other tab screen does not live-refresh until you navigate; writes within ~250 ms of each other can still race.
- The day strip shows the latest 21 days; older days through Archive.
- Mic/recognition tested with Chrome fake media device and a fake SpeechRecognition class; real recognition and Safari/Firefox behaviour are not verifiable here.
- The real pipeline day (G1) renders, but its content was not compared to sources (G7).
